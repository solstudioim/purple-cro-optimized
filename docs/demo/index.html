Purple CRO Optimized

A conversion-focused WooCommerce storefront

Watch the product journey, from discovery to checkout reassurance.

Storyboard text alternative

  1. Open a polished storefront with a clear promotional message.
  2. Browse product categories from the primary navigation.
  3. Find a product with the instant search experience.
  4. Review a product gallery, price, sale badge, and stock context.
  5. Read customer reviews and purchase reassurance near the buy box.
  6. Choose product variations and add the selected item to the cart.
  7. See the cart confirmation and free-shipping progress.
  8. Use the wishlist to save an item for later.
  9. Continue through a guided checkout with clear required fields.
  10. Finish with trustworthy policy guidance and post-purchase options.

Installation requirements

Install WooCommerce and the Purple parent theme, then activate Purple Optimize. Purple Optimize Toolkit is optional and adds store functionality.
